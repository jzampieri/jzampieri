### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** focused on **Backend Architecture**, **System Resilience**, and **Business Impact**.

Currently I work as a **Software Engineer at Amdocs**, engineering high-availability solutions for the **telecom sector across the CALA region** (Dominican Republic & Chile), building scalable **Java** backend services and cross-border system integrations in a multicultural, English-first environment.

Previously, I served as the **Technical Integration Manager (TIM) for OpenDelivery Brazil at Keeta (Meituan)**, leading complex enterprise integrations for one of the world's largest delivery platforms — bridging local partners with global stakeholders.

My engineering philosophy centers on **Clean Architecture**, **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, and building systems that solve real-world problems. I combine deep backend knowledge in **Java**, **C# (.NET)** and **Cloud (AWS)** with strategic communication skills (**English C1**).
